Method notes

    2 · Intro

    How to read the index

    Every quarter the analyst team reads the signals behind every deal in the market behind work: who is buying, who is raising, which leaders are moving, and what buyers are paying. The Signals Index turns 13,800+ tracked transactions and 2,700 2026 signals into one view of where the market is heating up and where it is cooling.

    Behind work means every market a business runs on, from backoffice to frontoffice: Work Tech software plus the tech-enabled services, outsourcing and professional services firms that keep work running. Each of 14 sectors and 11 industry verticals gets a grade built from three inputs.

    Deal volumeShare of all tracked deals in 2026 against the sector's 5-year average share. Shares, not raw counts, so changes in data coverage don't move the grade.
    Multiples2026 median EV/Revenue against the sector's own 5-year median. Needs 5 or more disclosed deals, otherwise held at Warm.
    Signals2026 leadership, product, funding, M&A and pressure signals matched to companies in the sector.
    A-Hot

    Hot on two inputs and Cool on none. Buyers are active and paying.

    B-Warm

    Mixed read. One input is running hot, or all three sit near the middle.

    C-Cool

    Cool on two or more inputs. Fewer deals, fewer signals or lower prices than history.

    Each input is ranked against the other sectors in thirds: top third Hot, middle Warm, bottom Cool. Multiples are also shown by tier, Public, Investor-Backed and Main Street, so owners and operators can see the gap between where they sit and where the premium sits, and which KPIs close it.

    Start with the Explorer and filter by sector, vertical, business model or buyer. Then go deeper into the pages that matter to your next move.
